# No More 9 to 5 — Affiliate Quick Start (1 page)

Welcome! Here's the fastest path to earning as an NM9t5 affiliate. (Full playbook:
*NM9T5 Affiliate Growth Framework*; AI shortcuts: *NM9T5 AI Prompt Pack*.)

## The one rule
**Every campaign has its OWN unique link and code (`am_id`).** Always copy the exact link for each
offer from your dashboard — never reuse one offer's code on another, or it won't credit you.

## 3 steps to your first promotion (≈30 minutes)

**1. Get your links.** Log in at `nomore9to5club.app.clientclub.net/affiliate/campaign` and copy
the link for each campaign. Start with these two (both ~33% commission, great for anyone):
- **30-Day Trial** → `…/b-m-checkout-30days-trial?am_id=YOURCODE`
- **NM9T5 Launchpad** → `…/launchpad?am_id=YOURCODE`
- (Free, no commission, lowest friction: the **Roadmap survey** at the homepage — good first touch.)

**2. Make a home base.** Create a free link-in-bio page (Linktree / Beacons / Carrd). Add your top
2-3 offers, one honest line each, with your links. Put high-commission + best-fit offers on top.

**3. Tell your people — honestly.** Post where it's genuinely relevant to your audience, and always
disclose: *"Affiliate link — I may earn a commission."* In groups, help first, promote only where
it fits, and follow their rules.

## Two ways to create the content

| By hand | With AI (faster) |
|---|---|
| Write a short caption: a hook, one real benefit, your link, a disclosure. Reuse it as an email line or a DM. | Paste a prompt from the **AI Prompt Pack** into ChatGPT/Claude with your links and audience; it writes captions, emails, or whole pages. You fact-check and post. |

## Match the offer to the person
- Thinking about leaving the 9-to-5 → Roadmap survey, then the 30-Day Trial
- Brand-new founder → Launchpad · Ready to commit → Trial → Membership
- Veteran/military spouse → free veteran training, then Trial
- Scaling an existing business → Delegation & Scaling / Done-For-You

## Want it to earn while you sleep?
Build a few honest web pages (a guide, an audience page, a fair comparison, a short answer page) —
search engines and AI assistants will send people to them for months. The **Growth Framework** and
**AI Prompt Pack** show you exactly how (or hand them to a developer/AI to build it).

## 4 things that keep you (and the network) safe
1. One unique code per campaign. 2. Disclose every affiliate link. 3. Never make up facts about
NM9t5 — check the live site. 4. Be honest and balanced, especially in comparisons.
