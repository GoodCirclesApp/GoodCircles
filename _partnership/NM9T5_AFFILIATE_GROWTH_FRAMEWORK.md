# The No More 9 to 5 Affiliate Growth Framework

A repeatable playbook **any NM9t5 member or affiliate** can model to promote the No More 9 to 5
Club with **their own affiliate codes** — by hand, with a website, or with an AI assistant doing
the heavy lifting. It generalizes a real, live deployment (Good Circles → NM9t5) so you're copying
something that works, not a theory.

> **Companion files**
> - `NM9T5_AI_PROMPT_PACK.md` — copy-paste prompts to make an AI do each step for you.
> - `NM9T5_PARTNER_INTEGRATION_TEMPLATE.md` — the deep technical spec (for a developer/AI agent
>   building a full website integration).
> - `PARTNER_INTEGRATION_CHECKLIST.md` — one-page quality checklist.

## STEP 0 — Get your own campaign links (everyone does this first)

**The single most important rule:** NM9t5 issues a **unique link per campaign** — *each campaign
has its own URL path AND its own `am_id` code.* There is **no single universal affiliate ID**. If
you paste one campaign's code onto another campaign's page, the referral won't credit you.

1. Log in to your affiliate dashboard: `nomore9to5club.app.clientclub.net/affiliate/campaign`.
2. For **each** campaign, copy its link **verbatim** (it already contains your `am_id`).
3. Put them in a personal **Link Sheet** (template below). Note each campaign's commission %.

## Choose your track

### TRACK A — Manual (no website required)

Best if you promote through social, email, DMs, a link-in-bio, or a community.

1. **Build a simple home base.** A free link-in-bio page (Linktree, Beacons, Carrd) listing 3-5 of
   your offers with one line each + your unique link. Put the high-commission, best-fit offers on top.
2. **Use the swipe copy** (in `NM9T5_AI_PROMPT_PACK.md` or write your own): a short version for
   social captions, a medium version for newsletters, a long version for a post or DM. Each ends
   with **your** unique link for that campaign.
3. **Post where it's genuinely relevant**, and **always disclose** you're an affiliate ("affiliate
   link — I may earn a commission"). In communities, *participate first*, mention NM9t5 only where
   it actually answers a question, and follow each group's self-promo rules.
4. **Repurpose, don't reinvent.** One idea → a caption, an email line, a short video script, a
   reply in a relevant thread.

### TRACK B — Website / content (the compounding SEO + GEO engine)

Best if you have (or will make) a website. This is what Good Circles did, and it's what keeps
earning while you sleep — search engines and AI assistants send people to your content for months.

Build a small **content cluster** (quality over quantity — a few excellent pages beat many thin ones):

1. **A cornerstone guide** (1,500–2,500 words) mapping the whole journey your audience is on, with
   your NM9t5 links placed at the right stages.
   *Model: goodcircles.org/learn/start-a-local-business*
2. **Audience pages** (~1,000 words) — one per group you serve (veterans, parents, creators, career
   changers…), each with an FAQ and one matched NM9t5 offer.
3. **Honest comparison/decision pages** — "NM9t5 vs traditional coaching," "best [category] programs
   2026" listing NM9t5 *fairly* alongside real alternatives. Balanced beats hype — AI engines and
   readers reward it, and it's the highest-value content for getting cited.
   *Model: goodcircles.org/learn/best-resources-for-veteran-entrepreneurs-2026*
4. **Short answer pages** (300–500 words) that open with a one-sentence answer, then expand — e.g.
   "What is the No More 9 to 5 Club?"
   *Model: goodcircles.org/answers/what-is-the-no-more-9-to-5-club*

For the build mechanics (affiliate-link helper keyed to your per-campaign codes, `rel="sponsored"`,
schema, sitemap, etc.) hand `NM9T5_PARTNER_INTEGRATION_TEMPLATE.md` to your developer or an AI
agent — it's the exact spec we used.

## The non-negotiables (these protect you AND the whole network)

- **One code per campaign.** Always use the campaign's own unique link from your dashboard. Never
  stamp one `am_id` onto a different offer's page.
- **Disclose every time.** FTC rules (and basic trust): label affiliate links/posts. Mark web links
  `rel="sponsored"`.
- **Never invent NM9t5 facts or quotes.** Pull details (offers, the founder, the MTP framework,
  prices) from the live site; don't make them up.
- **Be honest and balanced**, especially in comparisons. Name real strengths of alternatives. It
  wins trust, rankings, and AI citations.
- **No spam.** Participate before you promote; respect community rules.

## SEO + GEO essentials (Track B), in one screen

- **One `<h1>`**, a unique title (≤60 chars) and meta description (≤155) per page.
- **Answer-first:** put a direct 40–60-word answer at the very top of every page.
- **FAQ schema** (FAQPage JSON-LD) with the visible questions matching the schema text exactly —
  AI assistants extract these straight into answers.
- **Article + a partner `mentions`** of NM9t5 in JSON-LD; self-referencing canonical; an XML sitemap.
- **Internal links:** every page links to 3+ of your other pages.
- **Let AI in:** in `robots.txt`, allow GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended,
  Applebot-Extended. Add an `/llms.txt` summarizing your site + the NM9t5 partnership.
- **Freshness:** date your pages and refresh "2026"/"alternative" pages periodically.

## Measure and double down

- Tag your links with UTMs (`?…&utm_source=yoursite&utm_medium=cta&utm_campaign=…`) on top of the
  `am_id` so your own analytics show what's working.
- Watch your dashboard's **Clicks → Leads → Customers** per campaign. Move your best placements to
  your highest-converting offers; retire what doesn't move.
