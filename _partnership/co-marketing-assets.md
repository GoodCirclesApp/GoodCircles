# Co-marketing swipe copy — Good Circles (for NM9t5 + its members)

Paste-ready blurbs NM9t5 or any of its members can use to mention Good Circles. Each leads with the
**September 2026, Jackson MS first** hook and links the most relevant Good Circles page. Members can
add their own NM9t5 affiliate `am_id` to the Good Circles-bound link only if they are also a Good
Circles affiliate; otherwise link plain.

## For NM9t5's seller / entrepreneur audience → /for-business

**Short (X / SMS / caption):**
> Building a local business? Good Circles is a new marketplace where you keep **89% of your profit**
> (1% fee, not 15–30%). Launching Jackson, MS in Sept 2026, then nationwide. Founding Merchant
> spots are open: https://goodcircles.org/for-business

**Medium (newsletter blurb):**
> **Keep 89% of your profit when you sell local.** Good Circles is a community marketplace launching
> in the Jackson, MS metro in September 2026. Instead of the 15–30% the big apps take, it charges a
> 1% fee on profit — and your customers save about 10% and fund a local nonprofit when they buy from
> you. If you're building toward your first local sales, claim a free Founding Merchant spot (first
> 200 Mississippi businesses): https://goodcircles.org/for-business

**Long (blog / member spotlight):**
> If you're doing the work to start a business, where you sell decides how much you keep. **Good
> Circles** is a community marketplace launching in the Jackson, Mississippi metro in September 2026
> and expanding to the cities that request it most. The model is simple and on your side: you pay a
> **1% fee on profit** (so you keep about **89%**), your customers **save about 10%**, and **10% of
> your profit funds a local nonprofit** they choose — giving people a real reason to buy from you
> over a national chain. Good Circles even collects and remits sales tax for you as the marketplace
> facilitator. The first 200 Mississippi businesses become permanent Founding Merchants, and it's
> free to claim a spot. Start here: https://goodcircles.org/for-business
