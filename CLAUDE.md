## 1. PRODUCT SUMMARY

Good Circles is a community marketplace built on a triple-impact economic model. Neighbors save 10% automatically on every local purchase. Local businesses keep 89% of net profit while 10% routes automatically to the nonprofit their customers have elected. Nonprofits receive recurring, automatic revenue without fundraising. The platform takes 1% as its fee. For municipalities, it provides live local spend-retention data with no new infrastructure. For Treasury-certified CDFIs, it provides live merchant performance data in Qualified Investment Areas with TLR-ready packages. Launching Mississippi-first, September 2026.

**Manifesto line:** "You don't just get to use Good Circles. You get to build it."

## 2. FIVE AUDIENCE BRIEFS

### NEIGHBOR
- **One-line:** Shop local the way you already do, save 10% automatically, and fund your chosen nonprofit — no coupons, no apps, no extra steps.
- **Persona:** Mississippi resident who cares about community but has no practical financial reason to shop local over chains. Feels guilt-aware but budget-constrained.
- **Pain:** Only 13.6¢ of every chain-store dollar stays local. No reward for caring. Powerless against economic extraction.
- **Benefit:** Automatic 10% savings (~$480/year on $400/month spend). Every purchase funds elected nonprofit passively. 52.9¢ of every dollar stays local.
- **Hook:** Empowered — "the way I spend already is doing something."
- **CTA:** Join the founding circle at goodcircles.org
- **Visual vibe:** Warm, grassroots, neighborly

### BUSINESS (MERCHANT)
- **One-line:** Keep 89% of your net profit, gain community-loyal customers who are actively looking for local businesses, and make every sale a tax-deductible charitable contribution.
- **Persona:** Local Mississippi business owner tired of 15–30% platform fees. Margins collapsing on DoorDash/Amazon. Good at their craft, losing to infrastructure they don't control.
- **Pain:** Platforms take 15–30%. No discovery without paying for visibility. No customer loyalty mechanism.
- **Benefit:** 89% margin vs 70% on DoorDash/Amazon. 10% is tax-deductible charitable contribution. Founding Merchant badge is permanent. Community-loyal buyers actively seeking local.
- **Hook:** Vindicated — "the system finally works in my favor."
- **CTA:** Claim your Founding Merchant spot at goodcircles.org
- **Visual vibe:** Confident, entrepreneurial, community-proud

### NONPROFIT
- **One-line:** Receive a recurring share of local merchant profits automatically — no grant applications, no fundraising events, no asking anyone to be generous.
- **Persona:** Small-to-mid-size nonprofit director in Mississippi spending 1/3 of capacity writing grants. Knows Mississippi's generosity is real but the infrastructure puts the burden on the org, not the system.
- **Pain:** Average MS nonprofit grant: $5,000–$15,000, 2+ year timeline. 37% of nonprofits at a deficit. Donor fatigue is real.
- **Benefit:** 200 neighbors → ~$14,400/year automatically. 500 neighbors → ~$36,000/year. Scales passively with community. IRS-verified distributions. No ask required.
- **Hook:** Relief — "I can stop begging and start building."
- **CTA:** Claim your nonprofit's founding spot at goodcircles.org
- **Visual vibe:** Dignified, hopeful, mission-driven

### MUNICIPALITY
- **One-line:** Get real-time visibility into local spend retention and neighborhood-level business health — no new program, no consultant, no infrastructure to build.
- **Persona:** Economic Development Director or city/county official watching Main Streets decline with only lagging indicators to work with. Lacks live data to make the case to council.
- **Pain:** $8.6B leaves Mississippi annually through outside platforms. No real-time data on which neighborhoods/categories are growing. Every intervention requires new budget and infrastructure.
- **Benefit:** Daily spend-retention tracking. Neighborhood-level business category data. Dashboard plugs in — nothing to build.
- **Hook:** Equipped — "I finally have the data to take action."
- **CTA:** Request a founding partnership briefing at goodcircles.org
- **Visual vibe:** Civic, trustworthy, serious-but-hopeful

### CDFI
- **One-line:** See live revenue and transaction data on merchants in Qualified Investment Areas before they come looking for capital, with TLR-ready structured packages ready to submit.
- **Persona:** Treasury-certified CDFI loan officer whose pipeline runs on stale data, cold outreach, and self-reported PDFs. Mandated to lend in LMI census tracts but blind to live performance.
- **Pain:** No live visibility into QIA merchants. Cold outreach to find creditworthy borrowers is expensive. TLR prep is manual and disconnected.
- **Benefit:** Live revenue/margin/transaction data on LMI-tract merchants. Pre-engaged merchant relationships — no cold outreach. Structured merchant snapshots already mapped to TLR format.
- **Hook:** Clarity — "I can see what's actually happening before I decide."
- **CTA:** Request a founding partnership briefing at goodcircles.org
- **Visual vibe:** Professional, data-forward, civic

## 3. BRAND VOICE RULES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

- Bold, human, urgent — never corporate, never preachy, never utopian
- Names economic extraction without victimizing the people affected by it
- Specific over generic: real numbers, real places, real mechanisms
- Never use nonprofit guilt-language: no "give back," no "make a difference," no "join our community" platitudes
- Peer-to-peer tone: speak WITH neighbors, businesses, nonprofits, cities, CDFIs — not at them
- Plain words, short sentences, structural arguments
- Every claim with a number gets a citation in the source doc
- The CTA destination is always goodcircles.org — never any other URL
- "Founding Circle" / "Founding Merchant" / "Founding Nonprofit" / "Founding Partner" are permanent status terms — capitalize and use consistently
