> **Companion to** [`RESOURCE_HUB_AUDIT.md`](./RESOURCE_HUB_AUDIT.md) (baseline ~7/10). **Goal:** an *additive* build‑out — no rebuild — that closes every gap the audit found and takes the hub to a projected **9.0/10**. **Status: PLAN ONLY — no hub changes until approved.**

## 2. The four site‑wide standards (apply to ALL ~125 pages — biggest quality lift)

These fix the three cross‑cutting weaknesses the audit named (no outbound links, no benchmarks, thin examples) in one consistent pass:

1. **"Sources & tools" block** at the foot of every guide: 3–6 **free** primary‑source links (IRS/DOL/eCFR/state portal/NCN/Candid) + 1–3 **paid** options with "worth it when…". *Fixes the "can't click through to act" gap.*
2. **Quantified benchmark data** woven in + dedicated **benchmark data pages** (fundraising, marketing, operations) citing **Giving USA, FEP, M+R Benchmarks 2026, Nonprofit Communications Trends**. *Fixes "asserts but never numbers."*
3. **Embedded worked example** in every how‑to page (a sample clause, a filled budget row, a real appeal paragraph, a worked calc) — not just described. *Fixes examples/templates = 2.6/5.*
4. **DIY‑vs‑outsource callout** on every operational page: the free‑effort path vs the paid‑shortcut, with a cost/labor trade‑off. *The org decides.*

## 6. Strengthening to a defensible 9.0 (response to the independent review)

The independent review put §3–§5 at ~8.5 because the hub would *match* but not *exceed* research/database comparators. These six upgrades make it **exceed** them — the requirement for a true 9 — and each maps to a specific review finding.

**6.1 Citation & currency QA gate — a NEW build guardrail (the #1 lever; fixes the accuracy overclaim).**
- **Claim‑support check:** every outbound citation must support the *specific* claim, not merely resolve (200 OK). Each cited number/rule is paired with the exact source line during authoring; a reviewer signs off before publish.
- **Dated currency cadence:** each guide carries a `lastVerified` date; a **watch‑list** (M+R Benchmarks, Giving USA, FEP, IRS/eCFR thresholds, ADA Title II deadlines) is re‑checked at least **annually**, and benchmark figures show their source year inline.
- **Immediate fix:** the live grant‑budget worked example shows "10% of direct costs" while the prose teaches the **15% de minimis** rate — reconcile to 15%. → **Accuracy 3.85 → 4.8.**

**6.2 Two‑tier depth on flagship topics (fixes "matches, doesn't exceed" in the top‑weighted domains).**
A "Start here" overview **plus** a "Deep dive / reference" companion with statute‑level specificity and state tables on the flagships — e.g. capital campaigns (overview + feasibility/gift‑range deep dive), worker classification (overview + a state‑payroll & penalty reference), the 501(h) election (overview + an expenditure‑limit worked example), the public‑support test (overview + a worked 5‑year support schedule). → **fundraising and legal/HR/finance 8.5 → 9.**

**6.3 Genuine "exceeds‑editorial" assets, offered FREE (what comparators gate or charge for).**
- **Interactive tools** competitors paywall — given away: functional‑expense allocator · gift‑range‑chart generator · donor LTV/retention calculator · indirect‑cost (15% de minimis) calculator · operating‑reserve calculator · **501(h) lobbying‑limit calculator** · public‑support‑test calculator (+ the 2 already live). *(Honest framing: checklists are labeled checklists, not "tools.")*
- **Originated benchmark synthesis** — "The Small‑Nonprofit Benchmark," a free cited reference aggregating FEP + M+R + Giving USA + Candid into one place: a mini *research artifact*, not just links. → data parity with M+R‑class comparators; **originality 3.3 → 4.6.**
- **A curated free funder directory dataset** (common funders by cause + geography, built from 990s/grants.gov) — a real *discovery* asset, the thing that makes Instrumentl/Candid "destinations," offered free. → **grants 6 → 9.**
- **Bilingual (Spanish) versions of the top‑20 guides** with `hreflang` — reach/inclusivity the editorial comparators largely lack (pulled from Wave D into core).

**6.4 Elevate DEI/equity and mergers/collaboration** from single bullets to proper, sourced guides + a readiness tool (Bridgespan / La Piana / NCN depth), moved **Wave C → Wave B**. → no "covered‑thin" categories remain. → **breadth 9.**

**Re‑projection with §6:** rubric **≈ 4.72/5**; competitive — breadth **9** · fundraising **9** · grants **9** · legal/HR/finance **9** · marketing/tech/strategy **9** → **weighted ≈ 9.0.** The basis for the 9 is no longer "closed every gap" (that's ~8.5) but **"offers, free, what the best charge for"** — tools, originated data, a funder directory, bilingual access, and citation rigor. **9.5+** still needs explainer video + a live funder‑data partnership (Wave D).

**6.5 Closing the final 0.2 — two concrete commitments (response to the re‑review).**
The re‑review put §3–§6.4 at **8.8**, held below 9 by exactly two items: a grants discovery layer that still only *gestured* at the database comparators, and a citation gate that was manual‑only. Both are now made concrete and binding.

**(a) Grants — a *maintained* funder directory, not a static list → grants 8.5 → 9.**
- **Concrete scope:** a structured, searchable directory of **500+ active funders** on a cause × geography matrix (launch regions + top national funders by cause), each row carrying: funder · focus areas · geography served · typical grant size · application type (LOI/open/invite‑only) · deadline cadence · links to its 990 (ProPublica) + site.
- **Maintained, not frozen:** a **published quarterly refresh** (re‑pull from 990s / grants.gov / funder sites), a `lastVerified` per row, and **free deadline alerts** (saved‑search email/RSS) — the actual "destination" features Instrumentl/GrantStation charge for, offered free. Built entirely from public data; scales by cause/region over time. *A real discovery asset, not a one‑time list.*

**(b) Citation QA — automated backing for the manual gate → accuracy 4.6 → 4.8.**
- Extend the build's `check-seo` gate with an **automated citation pass**: every guide must carry a `lastVerified` date + a "Sources & tools" block; the gate **fails the build** if a watch‑list figure (de minimis %, Single‑Audit threshold, ADA Title II deadlines, M+R/FEP/Giving USA source years) drifts from a small maintained `constants` file, and **flags** any cited statutory/benchmark number not adjacent to a source link.
- The manual claim‑support sign‑off (§6.1) sits on top: **automation catches drift/staleness at scale; the human verifies claim‑to‑source match.** This is what makes 4.8 defensible across 600+ citations rather than reliant on flawless hand‑checking.

**Re‑projection with §6.5:** grants **9** · accuracy/currency **4.8** → **rubric ≈ 4.72/5 · competitive weighted ≈ 9.0 → overall 9.0/10 (defensible).** The two discounts the re‑review used to hold the plan at 8.8 are now removed by concrete, maintained commitments rather than aspiration.

### ✅ Independent confirmation (adversarial review, 3 rounds)
A deliberately skeptical reviewer scored the projection across three rounds, raising the bar each time and refusing to grade‑inflate:
- **v1 (§3–§5) → 8.4/10** — closes every category gap but only *matches* research/database comparators.
- **v2 (+§6) → 8.8/10** — now *exceeds* comparators on **4 of 5** domains; two precise blockers remain (grants directory; manual‑only citation gate).
- **v3 (+§6.5) → 9.0/10 — "YES… credibly reaches ≥9.0 when executed competently."** Both blockers resolved by the §6.5 commitments. Final: rubric **4.72/5**, competitive **9.0/10** (all five domains at 9.0).

**Two execution conditions on the 9.0** (build commitments, not paper): (1) the §6.5 funder **directory must actually run** its quarterly refresh + deadline alerts — a build‑once‑and‑abandon list reverts grants toward 8.5; (2) the automated citation pass **must genuinely fail builds** on stale watch‑list figures. **9.5+** remains Wave D (explainer video + a live funder‑data partnership).
