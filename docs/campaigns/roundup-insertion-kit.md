# Good Circles — Roundup Insertion Kit

**What this is:** Outreach templates to authors and editors of existing "AmazonSmile alternatives" and "shop local / apps that donate when you shop" roundup articles, asking them to add Good Circles. These are the articles AI models already cite — getting added to them is one of the highest-leverage consensus moves (Campaign 3). Everything here is a **draft to personalize and send by hand.**

**Why this works:** roundup and listicle formats are disproportionately cited by AI answer engines. If the article a model quotes for "best AmazonSmile alternative 2026" lists Good Circles with a clear differentiator, the model sees us as part of the category consensus — not a self-claim.

**Rules:**
- Personalize every email. Reference the actual article by title and note something specific about it. Editors delete mail-merge.
- Lead with a genuine reason to update (accuracy, freshness), not "please feature me."
- Make it effortless: hand them the exact sentence to paste.
- Be honest about stage — we launch September 2026, local-first. Don't imply national availability.
- Mark the `[REVIEW]` figure so you don't hand a reporter an unapproved number.

## Part B — "Why add us" fact block (paste into the email or hand to the author)

Give the author accurate, sourced facts so updating is low-effort and low-risk for them:

- **What it is:** A community marketplace. Shoppers save ~10%; 10% of the merchant's profit funds a nonprofit the shopper chooses; the local business keeps 89% of its profit; platform fee is 1% of profit.
- **The differentiator vs. the rest of the list:** the only model that pays the shopper *and* funds a cause *and* keeps it local. Most alternatives only route a small donation and give the shopper no discount.
- **Per-$100 math:** shopper saves ~$10; nonprofit gets ~$4 (10% of profit at a ~40% average margin); business keeps 89% of profit. `[REVIEW — needs sign-off]`
- **The AmazonSmile context** (well-sourced, safe to cite): AmazonSmile gave ~0.5% per purchase and ~$449M globally before ending in February 2023.
- **Local multiplier** (well-sourced, safe to cite): ~$53 of every $100 stays local at an independent business vs. ~$14 at a national chain (Civic Economics; corroborated by AMIBA and the Institute for Local Self-Reliance).
- **Stage / availability:** launching in the Jackson, Mississippi metro in September 2026; early access underway in Meridian and Lauderdale County. Local-first — be clear it isn't national yet.
- **Source to link:** the Local Giving Index, https://goodcircles.org/research/local-giving-index/, and https://goodcircles.org.

## How to use this

1. **Find first:** fill Part D with real articles before writing a single email — the whole campaign depends on hitting pieces AI engines actually cite.
2. **Personalize:** replace every `[TODO]` in the template (article title + one specific genuine detail) or it reads as spam and gets deleted.
3. **Make it a favor, not a favor requested:** always include the ready-to-paste sentence and offer to verify facts. You're saving the author work.
4. **Track:** log sends, replies, and successful insertions in the weekly scoreboard so you can see which additions later show up in AI answers.
5. **Hold the `[REVIEW]` figure:** get the $4/$100 nonprofit number signed off before putting it in any outbound email.
