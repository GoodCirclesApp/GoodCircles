# Good Circles Resource Hub — Explainer / Walkthrough Video Scripts

**Local production scripts — not for publishing online.** These are screen-recording walkthrough scripts for the Good Circles Nonprofit Resource Hub. Record the walkthroughs locally and keep the video files local (do not upload). Each script is self-contained: a shot list (what to click/scroll), timed voiceover narration, on-screen captions, a closing call-to-action, and recording notes.

**Before recording any video:**
- Open the live page logged out in a clean browser (no personal tabs/bookmarks, no org data on screen).
- Use only round, obviously-fake demo numbers in tools. Never show real EINs, donor/supporter data, financials, or any personal information.
- Speak every number and acronym aloud (accessibility) — the audio should stand on its own.
- Confirm every figure you say matches the live page that day; if the page changed, update the script before narrating.
- Keep Good Circles mentions exact: nonprofits earn **10% of the merchant's net profit** (not 10% of the purchase); shoppers save ~10% (estimate); free for nonprofits; ~$72 per active supporter/year is a conservative estimate (≈ $36,000 from 500); launch September 2026.

**Suggested recording order:** start with Video 1 (hub overview), then the tool demos (2–13), then the directory and templates (14–15), then the two cornerstone explainers (16–17).

## [VIDEO 2] Indirect Cost Calculator (15% de minimis) — 90-second demo

**[0:08]** Quick plain-English part. "Direct costs" are the things you spend straight on the project — staff time, supplies. "Indirect costs" are the shared costs of keeping your doors open, like rent and bookkeeping, that still keep the project running.

**[0:18]** Federal rules let many nonprofits recover indirect costs at a flat 15% rate — that's the "de minimis" rate in 2 CFR 200, and it means you don't need a negotiated agreement to use it. Let's try it. I'll put our total direct costs in here — two hundred thousand dollars.

**[0:52]** Watch the results. After we take those out, we're left with the MTDC base — that's "Modified Total Direct Costs," the slice the rate actually applies to. Ours is a hundred and forty thousand.

**[1:00]** Multiply by 15%, and we recover twenty-one thousand dollars in indirect costs. Add it all back together and our total project budget is two hundred twenty-one thousand.

**[1:12]** One thing worth saying out loud: nothing here is saved. We don't store your numbers — when you close the tab, they're gone. It's just a quick, private worksheet for you.

**[1:20]** That's it. Real numbers in, a defensible budget out, in under two minutes.
