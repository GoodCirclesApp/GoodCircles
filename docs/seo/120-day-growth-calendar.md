Plus: GA4 (audience-tagged conversion events), GSC + Bing submitted, an SEO regression gate in CI,
and a ready-to-send PR pack.

### Effectiveness rating
- **On-page / technical foundation: 9.5 / 10.** Essentially everything controllable on the site
  is done to an enterprise standard. The 0.5 is reserved for things that only emerge with data
  (CRO, query-driven content).
- **Off-page authority: ~1 / 10.** Zero backlinks, zero third-party mentions, zero AI-citation
  consensus. This is *expected* on day 0 — but it is now the binding constraint.
- **Current live organic performance: ~F (by design).** A brand-new domain with no authority and
  no age ranks for almost nothing yet. The site is a loaded engine that hasn't been fired.

**The one-line truth:** we have maximized what code can do. From here, growth is governed by three
things code cannot produce — **authority (links/mentions), time (indexing + aging), and the
September launch event.** The next 120 days are about those, not more pages.

## 3. The 120-Day SEO/GEO Calendar (aligned to the September 2026 launch)

> **Critical timing:** Good Circles launches **September 2026**. Day 1 ≈ Jun 12; **Day ~90 ≈ the
> launch**. So this calendar deliberately crescendos into launch and compounds after it. Pre-launch
> = build authority + indexing + demand. Launch = convert + seed + press. Post-launch = scale.

Legend: **[You]** = founder action · **[Me]** = I execute on request · **[Both]**.

### Month 1 — Days 1–30 (Jun 12 – Jul 12): INDEX & INSTRUMENT
*Goal: get the 275 pages crawled and indexed, start the local-link flywheel, baseline the data.*
- **[Me]** GSC URL-inspect + request indexing for the ~20 priority pages (flagship, 4 pillars,
  how-it-works, compare hub, MS hub + top 6 cities, top 8 learn). Fix any "not indexed" reasons.
- **[Me]** Internal-linking pass: ensure no orphan pages; strengthen links into the money pages.
- **[You]** Set up **Google Business Profile** for Good Circles (unlocks "near me"/Maps).
- **[You]** Submit consistent **NAP citations** to free directories (Crunchbase, local chambers,
  startup directories) — early easy backlinks + entity signals.
- **[You]** Confirm `press@goodcircles.org` works; send **PR Wave 1** (Jackson TV + MS papers — pitches ready).
- **[Both]** Give early founding merchants/nonprofits a "We're on Good Circles" badge linking to
  their city page — start local backlinks at scale.
- **KPIs:** % indexed (target 60%+), first GSC impressions, GBP live, 5–8 local pitches sent, first 3–5 referring domains.

### Month 2 — Days 31–60 (Jul 12 – Aug 11): AUTHORITY & CONSENSUS
*Goal: earn the mentions and backlinks that let pages actually rank — and that AI engines cite.*
- **[You/Me]** **PR Waves 2–3**: nonprofit + small-biz trades, then the **"best AmazonSmile
  alternatives" roundups** (highest-value GEO links — inclusion = backlink + AI consensus). I draft/personalize; you send.
- **[You]** Authentic **Reddit/community** participation (r/nonprofit, r/smallbusiness, r/mississippi) per the playbook; **1–2 podcasts/op-eds** (founder POV).
- **[Me]** **Freshness cadence:** 1–2 new `/learn` articles per week, now **driven by GSC query data** (write to what's already getting impressions).
- **[Me]** Diagnose programmatic indexing: if city pages are indexing → green-light expansion; if not → add unique data, batch indexing requests.
- **KPIs:** 15–25 referring domains, 2–4 listicle inclusions, first AI-citation appearances, rising impressions/positions on the priority clusters.

### Month 3 — Days 61–90 (Aug 11 – Sep 10): LAUNCH RUN-UP & LAUNCH
*Goal: convert the built demand at launch; flip Mississippi from "coming soon" to live + indexed.*
- **[Both]** **Seed the 21 MS city pages with real merchants/nonprofits** as founding signups land;
  set `realSeededEntries ≥ 6` → they auto-flip **noindex → index** + into the sitemap (the launch flywheel).
- **[Me]** Add `ItemList`/`LocalBusiness` schema + real listings to seeded city pages; build the live `/request-your-city` leaderboard.
- **[You/Me]** **Launch press push** (Release #1, the AmazonSmile-anniversary angle) + **reviews/Reddit/YouTube seeding** timed to launch.
- **[Me]** Begin **measured programmatic expansion** *only if* indexing is healthy: cause×city in controlled batches, "best AmazonSmile alternative in [state]" pages.
- **[Me]** Refresh top pages (dates, new launch facts) for freshness.
- **KPIs:** MS cities indexed + ranking locally, launch-press backlinks, first competitive-term page-1/2 movement, organic signups by audience climbing, request-your-city submissions accelerating.

### Month 4 — Days 91–120 (Sep 10 – Oct 10): COMPOUND & CONVERT
*Goal: turn the launch spike into a durable curve; optimize for signups, not just traffic.*
- **[Me]** **CRO pass** on the highest-traffic pages (CTA placement, A/B signup copy) using GA4 data.
- **[Both]** Scale the highest-ROI link sources (founding partners, chambers, MS partners) now that you have launch clips to cite.
- **[Me]** Expand the content clusters GSC shows winning; prune/fix any thin or non-indexing pages.
- **[Me]** Timely content tie-ins (back-to-school fundraising, Small Business Saturday prep for Nov).
- **[Both]** Establish the **ongoing operating cadence** for months 5–12 (the real payoff window).
- **KPIs:** organic sessions trend, **signup conversions by audience**, referring-domain total, AI-citation share on priority queries.

## 4. Projected performance WITH the calendar (day 120 ≈ ~1 month post-launch)

Ranges, not promises. "Expected" assumes solid execution of the off-page/PR work (which needs you).

| Metric | Conservative | **Expected** | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pages indexed (of 275) | ~180 | **~235** | ~275 |
| Referring domains | ~10 | **~25** | ~50 |
| Organic impressions / mo (GSC) | ~3k | **~12k** | ~35k |
| Organic clicks / mo | ~150 | **~700** | ~2,000 |
| Long-tail / local terms on page 1 | ~15 | **~40** | ~100 |
| "AmazonSmile alternative" cluster | page 4–5 | **page 2–3** | page 1 edge |
| AI-citation appearances (priority queries) | rare | **occasional/growing** | regular |
| Request-your-city submissions (cumulative) | ~150 | **~800** | ~3,000+ |
| Organic founding signups (cumulative) | ~50 | **~300** | ~1,000+ |

**Honest caveats:**
- Competitive head terms ("amazonsmile alternative," "passive fundraising") realistically reach
  page 1 in **6–12 months**, not 120 days — the calendar gets you *moving* on them, plus winning
  the long-tail and local now.
- The **single biggest swing factor is PR/link execution.** On-page is done; if the PR waves and
  community work don't happen, results track closer to the "no-work" column despite the great site.
- The **September launch is the inflection** — real merchants/nonprofits seed the MS city pages
  (unlocking local rankings) and create a genuine press moment. Miss the seeding and the local
  engine stays parked.
- The compounding **accelerates after day 120** — months 4–9 are where authority + aging convert
  the foundation into substantial traffic. Day-120 numbers are an early waypoint, not the ceiling.

### Bottom line
- **Foundation: A (9.5/10) — done.**
- **No further work:** the engine idles; near-zero compounding.
- **With the calendar:** by day 120 you should have real, measurable momentum (indexing near-complete,
  20–30 referring domains, hundreds of organic signups, the MS local engine live and ranking,
  first AI citations) — and, more importantly, a compounding curve heading into the high-growth
  window of months 4–12. The work that remains is **off-page and founder-dependent**, executed on a
  cadence — not more building.
