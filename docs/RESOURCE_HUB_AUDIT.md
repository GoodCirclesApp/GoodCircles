# Nonprofit Resource Center — Comprehensive Audit & Path to Best‑in‑Class

> **Date:** 2026‑06‑16 · **Scope:** the 75‑page Nonprofit Resource Center at `goodcircles.org/resources/`
> **Method:** a 15‑agent audit — 10 agents read every page in their category against a 5‑dimension rubric; 5 web‑enabled agents benchmarked the hub against the named best‑in‑class providers (Candid Learning, National Council of Nonprofits, Bloomerang, Bonterra/Network for Good, Donorbox, Instrumentl, Nolo, Harbor Compliance, Foundation Group, BoardSource, Propel Nonprofits, Nonprofit Marketing Guide, TechSoup, M+R Benchmarks, Bridgespan). Plus objective metrics (visible word counts).

---

## 1. Headline verdict

**The hub is a strong, opinionated, well‑built *intermediate starter library* — roughly the top quartile of free nonprofit resources — but it is not yet a *best‑in‑class reference***. It wins on structure, accuracy discipline, internal linking, real templates, and two interactive tools. It is held back by (a) **shallow depth** (most guides ~700–1,200 words vs 2,000–4,000+ at the leaders), (b) **thin embedded examples/assets**, (c) **no quantified benchmarks or outbound primary‑source links**, and (d) **6–8 whole topic areas that are missing entirely**.

| Lens | Grade | Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Coverage breadth** (vs the full nonprofit topic universe) | **B−** | strong on ~9 areas; ~7 whole areas absent |
| **Data quality** (accuracy, depth, actionability, originality, assets) | **B / B+** | rubric avg **3.4 / 5** |
| **Competitive standing** (vs best‑in‑class, weighted avg) | **6.4 / 10** | range 6–7 across 5 domains |
| **Overall** | **B (≈7/10)** | "excellent free starter library; not yet the definitive reference" |

**Bottom line:** the foundation is genuinely good. Reaching best‑in‑class (8.5–9/10) is an *additive* job — close the missing pillars and deepen what exists — not a rebuild.

## 2. Objective scale & depth metrics

- **75 pages · ~71,950 visible words · ~960 words/page average** (≈ a 250‑page book).
- **11 categories · 11 downloadable templates · 2 interactive tools** (grant‑readiness assessment, passive‑funding calculator).
- Depth by category (avg words/guide): **grants 1,227** · start‑a‑nonprofit 1,073 · marketing & program‑design ~1,026 · most others 800–940 · pillar index pages thin (~400–460).
- **Depth ceiling ≈ 1,200–1,550 words/guide.** Best‑in‑class reference guides (Nolo, Candid, NCN) routinely run 2,000–4,000+ words with state‑by‑state tables, form walk‑throughs, and primary‑source links.

## 3. Data‑quality scorecard (rubric, 1–5, averaged across categories)

| Dimension | Avg | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Actionability** | **3.90** | Strongest. Checklists, ordered steps, decision tables, self‑assessments. |
| **Accuracy / currency** | **3.85** | Substantively correct, consistently hedged ("as of 2026 — verify"), dated. *(One live defect found & fixed — see §6.)* |
| **Depth** | **3.35** | Explainer‑depth, not reference‑depth. Each topic stops at the conceptual layer. |
| **Originality** | **3.30** | Above generic AI boilerplate; the "build it grant‑ready/fundable from day one" through‑line is a genuine differentiator. |
| **Examples / templates** | **2.60** | Weakest. Few embedded worked examples; templates live in a separate library and aren't surfaced in‑context. |

**What's strong:** clean, consistent information architecture (answer box → TOC → H2s → callouts → checklist → FAQ mirrored in schema); correct, disciplined, well‑hedged facts (990 thresholds, 1023 fees, UBIT 3‑part test, Sarbanes‑Oxley nuance, fiscal‑sponsorship Model A/C); strong SEO/GEO; an honest practitioner voice (e.g., events guide's "measure net, not gross — count staff hours").

**What's weak:** (1) **no outbound links to primary authorities** — pages name the IRS, Pay.gov, Pub 78, the URS, state regulators but a reader can't click through to act; (2) **no quantified benchmarks** — retention rates, email $/1k, cost‑to‑raise‑a‑dollar, conversion rates are asserted as "higher/more" but never numbered or cited; (3) **load‑bearing examples are described but not shown** — sample exempt‑purpose & dissolution clauses, a sample appeal letter, a gift‑range chart, a filled budget, a sample 990 excerpt; (4) **thin pillar index pages** (~280–460 words, pure navigation).

### 5C. Depth & data gaps on EXISTING pages (don't add pages — deepen these)

- **No outbound primary‑source links anywhere** (IRS.gov forms, Pay.gov, state portals, Pub 1771/526, M+R, Giving USA). High‑impact, low‑effort. **P1**
- **No quantified benchmarks** — add a "How does my org compare?" data page/table: donor retention (~45% overall, ~60–63% repeat, ~19% new — FEP/AFP), email $54/1k (M+R), sustainer ≈27% of online revenue, cost‑to‑raise‑a‑dollar, ROAS ≈$2.48. **P1**
- **Embed worked examples**: a verbatim exempt‑purpose + dissolution clause; a sample first‑year startup budget; a sample appeal & thank‑you letter; a filled gift‑range chart; a logic‑model row with the assumptions/external‑factors line; a public‑support‑test calculation. **P1**
- **Grants depth**: a repeatable prospect‑research methodology, a 12‑month grants calendar/pipeline, NICRA/indirect‑cost negotiation, evaluation‑plan design, funder‑relationship building, "what to do after a no." **P1**
- **Finance depth**: functional‑expense allocation & the Statement of Functional Expenses, chart of accounts (UCOA), cash vs accrual, audit preparation & thresholds, investment/spending policy. **P1**
- **Thicken the 11 thin pillar index pages** (~280–460 words) into real overview/landing content. **P2**

### 5D. Template library gaps (~18 standard templates missing; competitors ship these free)

Donation/gift‑acknowledgment **receipt** (IRS‑compliant: $250 written ack, $75 quid‑pro‑quo, EIN) · **gift acceptance policy** · **whistleblower policy** · **document retention/destruction policy** · **chart of accounts (UCOA)** · **board recruitment / skills matrix** · **annual fundraising/development plan** · **gift range chart** · **annual report** (currently "Coming soon") · **volunteer agreement** · **expense reimbursement / accountable‑plan policy** · **donor survey** · **ED/staff performance review** · **operating‑reserve policy** · **12‑month cash‑flow worksheet** · **MOU / partnership agreement** · **email appeal / ask letters** (only thank‑yous exist) · **sponsorship one‑pager** · **990 prep checklist**.

### 5E. Structural ceiling (what makes the leaders "destinations")

- **No discovery/database layer** (Instrumentl's 490k funders, Candid's Foundation Directory, GrantStation's grantmaker DB). The hub is pure editorial. A curated directory or partnership would lift the grants domain materially.
- **No annotated real‑world artifacts** (Candid's free sample‑document library of winning proposals/990s/budgets).
- **No credentialing / skills‑progression track** (GPA/GPCI for grants; NonprofitReady courses).

## 6. Accuracy fixes

- **✅ FIXED (this audit):** the grant‑budget guide stated a **10%** federal **de minimis indirect cost rate**; the current rate is **15% of MTDC** (2 CFR 200 revision, effective Oct 1, 2024). Corrected in all 3 places.
- **Verify/refresh:** Single Audit threshold ($750k → **$1,000,000** for FYs beginning on/after Oct 1, 2024) — the federal‑grants page wisely says only "a federal threshold," so no wrong number, but could state the current figure.
- **Add the $75 quid‑pro‑quo disclosure rule** to the receipt/acknowledgment guidance (fundraising + donor pages currently stop at the $250 written‑ack rule).
- **Cite benchmark claims** (retention rates, "individuals ≈ two‑thirds of US giving") to Giving USA / FEP rather than asserting them.

## 7. Roadmap to best‑in‑class

**Target: move 6.4 → 8.5+/10.** Sequenced by impact‑per‑effort.

### Phase A — Close the HIGH‑importance holes (biggest rating lift)
New pillars/guides (~14–18 pages): **HR & Employment** (worker classification, payroll, handbook, hiring/firing) · **Advocacy & 501(h)** · **Cybersecurity & data privacy** · **Web accessibility (WCAG/ADA)** · **Capital campaigns + gift‑range chart** · **Planned & legacy giving** · **Executive leadership & succession**. *Each lift moves a domain ~1–2 points.*

### Phase B — Deepen & cite what exists (quality lift across all 75)
Add **outbound primary‑source links** site‑wide · ship a **quantified benchmarks** page · **embed worked examples** (sample clauses, letters, gift‑range chart, budgets) · deepen **grants** (prospect research, calendar, NICRA) and **finance** (functional expense, chart of accounts, audit prep) · thicken pillar index pages.

### Phase C — Ship the missing assets & advanced tracks
Build the **~18 missing templates** (above) + the "Coming soon" annual report · add **P2P/crowdfunding**, **GivingTuesday playbook**, **DAF/matching/in‑kind** guides · **DEI/equity**, **mergers/capacity**, **"staying tax‑exempt"** (4958/exec comp/inurement) · explore a **curated funder directory** or database partnership.

### Effort & outcome
- **To ~8/10 (clear best‑in‑class for small/emerging nonprofits):** Phase A + Phase B ≈ **15–20 new pages + depth upgrades to ~20 existing pages + outbound links**.
- **To 8.5–9/10 (definitive reference):** add Phase C ≈ **30–45 new pages total + ~18 templates + a discovery layer**.
- The existing template + voice + IA are reusable, so new pages can ship at the current quality bar quickly.
