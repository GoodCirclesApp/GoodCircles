Mississippi · State Playbook

Start & run a nonprofit in Mississippi

Updated June 17, 2026 · Good Circles · the Mississippi-specific layer
🌐 Leer en español →
National guides tell you what every U.S. nonprofit must do; this playbook tells you where and how in Mississippi. It sequences the state-specific steps — incorporate with the Secretary of State, register to solicit donations, file the annual report, handle state tax, and register as an employer — each with the fee, the deadline, and a link to the official Mississippi agency. General information, not legal advice; confirm current fees on the official site before filing.

How to Incorporate a Nonprofit in Mississippi →
