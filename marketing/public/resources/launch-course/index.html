Launch & grow your nonprofit in 12 months

Free · one lesson a month · earn a certificate of completion
Starting a nonprofit can feel overwhelming. This free course breaks it into one focused step a month — from sharpening your mission to incorporating, getting 501(c)(3) status, raising money, and growing — each lesson built on free Good Circles guides, tools, and templates. By month 12 you'll have a registered, funded, growing organization, and a certificate to prove it.

Why Good Circles built this

Good Circles is free for nonprofits, and it's how we help fund the work you're about to build: supporters pick your cause once, then a share of their everyday local spending — about 10% of each merchant's net profit, conservatively ~$72 per active supporter per year (an estimate) — funds you automatically, recurring and unrestricted. We launch Mississippi-first in September 2026.
