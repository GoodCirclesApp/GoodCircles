Marketing & Communications

Free marketing your nonprofit status unlocks.

A 501(c)(3) designation does more than make gifts tax-deductible — it unlocks an enormous amount of free marketing: $10,000 a month in Google ads, donated software, free design tools, and channels most businesses pay for. Here's how to claim all of it.

A registered 501(c)(3) is a marketing asset, not just a tax status. Tech companies, ad platforms, and software vendors run nonprofit programs that hand verified charities tools worth thousands of dollars a month — for free. The work isn't paying for reach; it's claiming the reach already set aside for you, then telling a story worth following.
