Why these templates are free

Good Circles funds nonprofits — passively, automatically, forever.

We give these tools away because well-equipped nonprofits build stronger communities. Good Circles is the marketplace that turns everyday local spending into recurring, unrestricted funding for the causes people choose — about $72 per active supporter per year (≈ $36,000/year from 500 supporters), free for your nonprofit to join. No fundraising required.
