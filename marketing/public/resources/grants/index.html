Grants & Institutional Funding

Win grants by being the kind of nonprofit funders fund.

Grants don't go to the organizations that need money most — they go to the ones that look like a safe, well-run bet. Get grant-ready, find the right funders, and write proposals that win, with the structure reviewers actually reward.

Most grant money is decided before a proposal is even written. Funders back nonprofits with confirmed 501(c)(3) status, real governance, measurable programs, and diversified, durable revenue. How you structure your organization — and design your programs — determines how much grant money you can unlock. These guides walk that whole path, from readiness to a funded ask.

Three things separate funded nonprofits from the rest. First, structure: a clean compliance record, a working board, and finances a stranger can trust. Second, program design: clear outcomes, a logic model, and evidence that what you do actually creates the change you promise. Third, durability: recurring, unrestricted income that proves you won't collapse if one grant falls through. Get those right and proposal-writing becomes the easy part — you're just documenting a story that's already true.
